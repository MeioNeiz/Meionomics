# Meionomics

## 1.2.0

- Meionomics tab in the Auction House: Browse (every auction with its seller, % of market,
  deals below market or vendor price), Patterns (recipes up now, worth learning or not),
  Sell (your bag items worth posting, one key press per post), Shopping (buys what the craft
  queue still needs) and BiS upgrades (gear that beats what you wear, with Forever Leveling
  BiS)
- Sells in a day: a posting price that still sells within a day, learnt from your own posts
- Worth window: what every character's bags, bank, gear, mail and auctions are worth
  (`/meio worth`)
- Merchant buttons: buy the craft queue's materials a vendor sells, sell junk
- Working Overtime: skill-up chances and routes count the Legacy talent (`/meio legacy`)
- Key bindings: craft next, craft one, open the panel, post the next Sell row
- `/meio tooltips off` (or Settings) leaves item and trainer tooltips alone
- TSM fix 12 (Enchanting window) removed: TSM fixed it
