# Meionomics

## 1.4.0

- Invest (Flips tab): say how much gold you'd put in a day and see what to buy or craft for
  the most back, beside what TSM's ledger says you made with your gold
- Train queue recipes: a button under a trainer's window learns the recipes your queue
  still needs that this trainer teaches, in queue order, as far as your gold goes
- Bank, mailbox and guild bank mats each count or not for the queue and routes (Queue tab
  menu, Settings, `/meio craft bank|mail|guild`)
- The shopping list stops where the AH or your gold runs out, and says what is short
- The shopping list buys a queued recipe's pattern when it's on the AH (or for less than
  the trainer's fee); Craft next learns it from your bags when its turn comes
- The queue no longer buys or makes again what earlier queued crafts make, buys a missing
  tool when that is cheaper than making it, and crafts each step's materials just before it
- Routes: mats you hold make a step cheaper ("own -X"), and your alts' professions and your
  other one count too: mats another route needs say whose, so you can mail them
- Use my mats first: your mats count at what they'd sell for, and it stops where the route
  levels for less
- Sniper bets on the lower of market and region sales, so a realm market far over what
  sells no longer makes a big deal
- Picking a row in the Meionomics tab opens its price history beside the AH
- Patterns the game hadn't loaded yet show their names instead of "item 1234"
- The AH tab's status line no longer runs under the buttons; hover a cut one to read it all

## 1.3.0

- Flips (`/meio flips`): crafting chains that make money, using every character's recipes:
  materials bought or crafted the cheapest way, the result sold, vendored or disenchanted,
  ranked by profit a day
- Sniper in the AH tab, and deal alarms (`/meio snipe`): items under a share of market,
  under vendor price or under your own max price, with a sound and a raid warning
- A few stray cheap listings no longer set an item's price
- New patterns worth learning come as one chat list per scan, best first, in a chat tab
  named Meionomics if you have one
- Sell junk never sells gear, and follows Meio Bags' junk and keep lists
- Shopping: buying part of a listing leaves the rest of it to buy
- Working Overtime: each character's own bonus when viewing an alt's professions
- Fixed an error at vendors inside dungeons
- Sell tab: a price box you clear to retype stays clear while searches answer
- Sell tab: the next bag of an item gets the price you just posted it at, until you post at
  the suggested price or close the AH; a row you pick from the list keeps its price
- New logo

## 1.2.0

- Meionomics tab in the Auction House: Browse (every auction with its seller, % of market,
  deals below market or vendor price), Patterns (recipes up now, worth learning or not),
  Sell (your bag items worth posting, one key press per post), Shopping (buys what the craft
  queue still needs) and BiS upgrades (gear that beats what you wear, with Forever Leveling
  BiS)
- Sells in a day: a posting price that still sells within a day, learnt from your own posts
- Worth window: what every character's bags, bank, gear, mail and auctions are worth
  (`/meio worth`)
- Merchant buttons: buy the craft queue's materials a vendor sells, sell junk
- Working Overtime: skill-up chances and routes count the Legacy talent (`/meio legacy`)
- Key bindings: craft next, craft one, open the panel, post the next Sell row
- `/meio tooltips off` (or Settings) leaves item and trainer tooltips alone
- TSM fix 12 (Enchanting window) removed: TSM fixed it
